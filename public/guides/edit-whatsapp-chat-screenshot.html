How to Edit a WhatsApp Chat Screenshot

Photo editors are painful for this — line up a font, match the green, fake a tick, and it still looks off. The clean way to “edit” a WhatsApp screenshot is to rebuild the chat and change the parts you want: the contact name, the message text, the timestamps, even grey vs. blue ticks. Then export a fresh, pixel-perfect image. Here’s how.

✏️ Edit your chat

Why rebuild instead of photoshop

  • Real bubbles, real font. No mismatched text or fuzzy edges — it renders the actual chat UI.
  • Ticks that look right. Proper single/double/blue ticks instead of a pasted shape.
  • Easy timestamps. Change a time or date with a tap instead of cloning pixels.
  • Re-editable. Change your mind and re-export — no flattened layers to fight.

Step by step

  1. Open Chat TreeGo to chattreeapp.fun. Start a fresh chat, or choose Upload exported chat to edit a real conversation you’ve exported.
  2. Turn on Edit modeFlip the Edit switch — now every message, time and tick is editable.
  3. Edit the messagesTap a bubble to change its text. Add new messages, delete ones you don’t want, and set who is sending each.
  4. Change name, time & ticksSet the contact name and avatar at the top. Tap a timestamp to change the time, tap a day divider to change the date, and tap the ticks to switch sent / delivered / blue read.
  5. Export a fresh imageExport the result as a sharp PNG or a PDF — clean, with none of the tell-tale signs of a photo edit.

What you can change

  • Message text — reword or replace any bubble.
  • Contact name & photo — set who the chat is “with”.
  • Times & dates — per-message time, and the date on each day divider.
  • Ticks — single grey (sent), double grey (delivered), double blue (read).
  • Who sent it — flip a message to the other side of the chat.
  • Extras — add replies, reactions, forwarded labels, call logs and system notices.
  • Look — light or dark mode, phone frame and status bar on or off.
An honest note: editing chats is great for memes, mockups, tutorials and fixing your own screenshots. Please don’t edit a chat to fake evidence, impersonate a real person, or mislead or harm anyone — that’s not what this is for.

FAQ

Can I change the time and date?

Yes — tap a message’s timestamp to change its time, and tap a day divider to change the date.

Can I turn grey ticks blue?

Yes. Tap the ticks on your messages to cycle single grey (sent) → double grey (delivered) → double blue (read).

Can I edit a chat I exported from WhatsApp?

Yes — upload the _chat.txt or .zip, turn on Edit mode, and change the text, names, times and ticks before exporting.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any modern mobile or desktop browser, no app to install.

Is editing private?

Yes. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to save the chat to your account.
