How to Make a Funny Fake WhatsApp Chat for a Prank

A well-made fake chat screenshot is a classic harmless prank — a “celebrity replied to me”, an outrageous group message, a wrong-number gone hilarious. Here’s how to build a believable one in a minute, the little touches that sell it, and how to keep the joke friendly so everyone ends up laughing.

😄 Make your prank chat

Step by step

  1. Open Chat TreeGo to chattreeapp.fun and choose Create your chat — a blank WhatsApp-style conversation opens.
  2. Write the jokeTurn on Edit, type the messages on both sides, and pick who is sending each one. Build up to the punchline so the last visible bubble delivers it.
  3. Make it believableSet a contact name and photo, drop in a photo or an emoji reaction, and quote a message as a reply if it helps the bit.
  4. Nail the little detailsChoose blue read ticks, and tap a time to change it so the replies aren’t all the same minute. Real chats have gaps.
  5. Export & shareExport a sharp PNG and send it to your friend — then reveal the joke soon after.

Prank ideas that land

  • The “famous person replied”. A short, dry reply from a celebrity or brand account name.
  • The wrong-number spiral. A stranger who is weirdly, hilariously persistent.
  • The group-chat chaos. Set it as a group and let three “people” talk over each other.
  • The impossible flex. A screenshot of plans no one would ever actually agree to.
  • The cliffhanger. End on a “delivered” but unread grey ✓✓ so it looks like they left you hanging.

Tips to make it look real

  • Vary the timing. Space some replies a few minutes apart — an evenly-timed chat reads as fake.
  • Use ticks with intent. “Delivered” grey ✓✓ but not read is often funnier than everything blue.
  • Keep it short. Two or three punchy bubbles beat a wall of text — the eye reads a screenshot in a second.
  • Match the mode. Use dark mode if your friends’ chats are dark, so it blends in.
  • One photo sells it. A single image with a short caption makes the scene believable.
Keep it kind: a fake chat is great for a laugh you both share. Please don’t use one to impersonate a real person, fake evidence, spread a rumour, or scare, bully or trick anyone into a real decision. A good prank is revealed quickly and hurts no one — that’s the whole difference.
