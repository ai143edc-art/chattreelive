How to Make a Fake WhatsApp Chat (Free, Online)

Want a realistic WhatsApp-style conversation for a meme, a tutorial screenshot, a design mockup or just for fun? Here is the quick way to build one in your browser — with messages on both sides, photos, reactions and those blue ticks — and export it as an image or PDF. No app to install, and no sign-up to try it.

🚀 Make your chat now

Step by step

  1. Open Chat TreeGo to chattreeapp.fun and choose Create your chat. It opens a blank WhatsApp-style conversation you can fill in.
  2. Turn on Edit modeFlip the Edit switch on. Now you can add, change and delete any message on either side of the chat.
  3. Add the messagesType a message, pick whether you or the other person is sending it, and hit add. You can also drop in photos and videos, quote a message as a reply, add emoji reactions, and insert call logs or the “Messages are end-to-end encrypted” notice.
  4. Make it look realSet the contact’s name and profile photo, choose the phone model and status bar, and for each of your messages pick a single grey tick (sent), double grey (delivered) or double blue (read). Tap a time to change it so the timeline feels natural.
  5. Export itDownload the whole chat as a sharp PNG image or a PDF. You can even turn a long conversation into a printable keepsake book.

Tips for a believable chat

  • Mix the timing. Real chats aren’t evenly spaced — leave a few minutes between some replies and cluster others together.
  • Use blue ticks sparingly. A message that’s “delivered” (grey ✓✓) but not yet “read” often looks more real than everything being blue.
  • Add one or two photos. A single image with a short caption breaks up the text and sells the scene.
  • Keep the names consistent. Set the contact name once at the top so the header and bubbles match.
  • Group short replies. Two quick messages from the same person in a row look natural — Chat Tree groups them automatically.
