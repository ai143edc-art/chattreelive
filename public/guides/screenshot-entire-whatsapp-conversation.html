How to Screenshot an Entire WhatsApp Conversation (Long Chats)

Trying to save a whole conversation the normal way means dozens of phone screenshots — with bubbles cut in half at every edge, and no easy way to stitch them together. There’s a cleaner way: turn the whole chat into a single long image or a tidy PDF, top to bottom, nothing sliced. Here’s how.

Why phone screenshots don’t work for long chats

  • Cut-off bubbles. Every screenshot slices a message at the top and bottom edge.
  • Dozens of files. A long chat becomes 30, 50, 100 images you then have to order and stitch.
  • Wasted chrome. Each shot repeats the status bar, header and keyboard — clutter you don’t want.
  • No search. You can’t find one line across a pile of screenshots.

The clean way: export, then capture the whole thing

The trick is to first get the conversation out of WhatsApp as a file, then let a viewer rebuild and capture it in one go.

  1. Export the chat from WhatsAppOpen the conversation, use Export chat, and save it — choose with media if you want the photos in the capture too. (Full steps: how to export a WhatsApp chat.)
  2. Open it in Chat TreeGo to chattreeapp.fun, choose Upload exported chat, and drop in the _chat.txt or .zip.
  3. Check it top to bottomThe whole conversation loads as a real-looking WhatsApp chat you can scroll and search — no missing pieces.
  4. Export as image or PDFUse the export menu to save the entire chat as one long PNG image or a multi-page PDF. Bubbles are split at message boundaries, so nothing is chopped in half.

Image, PDF, or keepsake book?

  • One long image (PNG) — best for sharing a shortish chat in one shot, or for a screenshot you’ll crop.
  • PDF — best for a long conversation: it flows across clean pages you can save, print or email.
  • Keepsake book — for something you want to keep, the book export adds a cover, chapters and page numbers. See save a WhatsApp chat as a PDF or keepsake book.

Tips for a clean capture

  • Export with media if you want the photos to appear inline — a text-only export leaves them out.
  • Turn the phone frame off for a plain document look, or leave it on to keep the WhatsApp feel.
  • Very long chat? Use the PDF or book export rather than one giant image — a single image has a maximum height, but paged exports don’t.
  • Pick light or dark before exporting so every page matches.
Private by default: uploading, viewing and exporting all happen in your browser. Your conversation isn’t sent to a server unless you deliberately save a chat to your account.

FAQ

Will messages get cut in half?

No — the export splits at message boundaries, so bubbles are never sliced across the edge of an image or page.

How long a chat can it handle?

Very long ones. The PDF and book exports render page by page, so thousands of messages export fine without the single-image height limit that breaks one giant screenshot.

Does it include photos and videos?

Yes, if you exported the chat with media (a .zip). Images show inline just like the real chat.

Can I still search the chat?

Yes — once it’s loaded you can scroll and search the whole conversation before you export it.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to save a chat to your account.
