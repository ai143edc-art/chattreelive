How to Save WhatsApp Chats as a PDF or Keepsake Book

Some conversations you never want to lose — with someone you love, a family group, or a chat full of memories. Chat Tree lets you take a whole WhatsApp conversation and keep it forever: as a clean PDF, or as a printable keepsake book with a bookcloth cover, your photos and every blue tick, exactly as it looked.

📖 Make your book now

Step by step

  1. Export the chat from WhatsAppOpen the chat in WhatsApp → tap the ⋮ menu → More → Export chat. Choose Include media so your photos and videos come along. You’ll get a .zip (or a _chat.txt) file.
  2. Upload it to Chat TreeGo to chattreeapp.fun → Upload a chat → drop in the file. Chat Tree rebuilds the conversation with the real WhatsApp look — bubbles, media, replies, reactions, dates and ticks.
  3. Choose PDF or a bookFor a simple copy, export it straight to PDF. For something to keep or gift, open Book Studio.
  4. Design the keepsake bookPick a cloth cover and foil colour, a page size, turn on month chapters and a contents page, add a title and dedication — then export. On a long chat, switch on “2 columns / page” so it builds faster with no loss of quality.

What the keepsake book includes

  • A bookcloth cover with a foil-stamped title, your names and the date range
  • Six cloth themes and seven page sizes (A4, A5, 6×9in, square, and more)
  • Chapters by month, a contents page and a page number on every leaf
  • Your messages printed exactly as you see them — ticks, replies, reactions, photos
  • A clean PDF you can print at any photo-book service, or simply keep

Nice ways to use it

  • A memory book of chats with a partner or best friend
  • Preserving a family group’s messages over the years
  • Keeping the messages of someone you’ve lost
  • A milestone conversation — a proposal, good news, a first hello
Private by design: your chat is read and turned into a book right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to save it to your own account.

FAQ

Does the book keep my photos?

Yes — export the chat with media included and the photos are placed into the pages just as they appeared.

Can I get it printed?

Yes. It exports as a PDF in standard sizes, so any print or photo-book service can produce it — or you can just keep the PDF.

Is it free?

You can build and export your book for free in your browser.
